⚙️ Engineering Formulas

Conduction Heat Load

Q̇ = (A/L) × ∫T_coldT_warm k(T) dT   [W]

Inputs: Cross-section area A [m²], Length L [m], Tcold [K], Twarm [K]

Cooldown Energy

E = m × ∫T₁T₂ cp(T) dT   [J]

Inputs: Mass m [kg], T₁ [K], T₂ [K]

Thermal Strain

ΔL = Lref × [Y(T₂) − Y(T₁)] × 10⁻⁵   [m]

Inputs: Reference length Lref [m], T₁ [K], T₂ [K]

Series Thermal Resistance

Rtotal = Σᵢ (Lᵢ / (ki,avg × Aᵢ))   [K/W]

Per layer: Length L, Average conductivity kavg, Area A

🔢 Integration Methods

Composite Trapezoid

O(h²)

Baseline reference method. Simple, robust.

Simpson's 1/3 Rule

O(h⁴)

Fixed step count. Better accuracy than trapezoid.

Romberg Integration

Adaptive

Richardson extrapolation to machine precision. Reference standard.

Gauss-Legendre 4pt

O(h⁸)

4-node Gaussian quadrature per subinterval. Highest order.
